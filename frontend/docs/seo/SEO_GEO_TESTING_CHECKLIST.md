## 8. How to judge results

| Result | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Brand search shows Alcoa | Good | Maintain GBP + site |
| `site:` shows pages | Indexed | Continue monitoring |
| Money keyword on page 2–5 | Early progress | Improve content/reviews; wait |
| Money keyword not visible yet | Normal early | Keep GSC indexing + GBP active |
| AI mentions wrong NAP | Bad | Fix listings/GBP consistency |
| AI does not mention Alcoa yet | Normal early | Grow reviews + citations |
