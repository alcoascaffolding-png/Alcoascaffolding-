# Monthly Ongoing SEO / AEO Checklist

## Content

- [ ] Publish 1–2 blog posts (informational UAE keywords)
- [ ] Update one service or location page with fresh photos or FAQs
- [ ] Internal links from new content → `/services/rental`, `/faq`, `/contact-us`

## Google Business Profile

- [ ] 4 weekly posts (offer / product / educational / review)
- [ ] Respond to all reviews within 24 hours
- [ ] Ask for reviews after completed jobs (WhatsApp script in GBP_CHECKLIST.md)

## Citations & links

- [ ] Add 2–3 quality directory or partner mentions
- [ ] Pitch one guest/safety article if capacity allows
- [ ] Verify NAP still matches `VERIFIED_FACTS.md` on top 10 listings

## Measurement

- [ ] GSC: top 20 queries, CTR, average position
- [ ] Note pages with impressions but low CTR → rewrite title/meta
- [ ] Confirm new URLs indexed

## Do not

- Buy cheap link packages
- Publish unverified AED price tables or fake review counts
- Claim “#1 / best company” without approved comparison content
